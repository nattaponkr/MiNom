## Workflow

1. Pick someone from your circle who fits the criteria; add a row.
2. Send the warm-intro DM from `BETA_COMMS.md` §1 (personalized — fill in the `[เหตุผลเฉพาะตัว]` bracket honestly).
3. Update status to `📩 intro sent`.
4. If they say yes, ask for the email they want to use for ละมุน. Status → `✅ yes — email captured`.
5. If they take >7 days to respond and you've nudged once, mark `🚫 no response` and move on.
6. When PM sends the invite from the app, update to `🌿 invited`.
7. After they sign up and start logging, update to `🟢 active`.

When you hit **20 active**, stop recruiting and focus on supporting them.

---

## Where to look (channels)

- **Your own network first.** Friends and friends-of-friends with newborns.
- **LINE openchats for new parents** (with care — ask the admin first; bulk-pasting in groups burns goodwill).
- **Pediatric clinic relationship** if you have one — warm intro from a doctor is the highest-quality recruitment channel.
- **Parents you've met casually** (school reunions, work circles) — surprisingly effective.
- **Snowball:** every household who says yes can refer 1–2 more if they think a friend would like it.

If you hit 15 days with <15 yeses, flag PM — we may need to broaden the criteria or sweeten the ask.
